## Introduction

Ever since my views on capitalism and private property began to change, I have wondered
fairly extensively about the implications of these systems on others. For much of my life
I've held fairly progressive beliefs and been surrounded by very progressive people, many of
which also espouse socialism or bring up a random socialist talking point in conversation
(eg. "late stage capitalism.")

But, after reading, listening, and pondering a lot, I've come up with what I think is a perspective
which is quite rare. Capitalism is very often associated with the conservative right. In my view, though,
it could just as easily be thought of as the only system which can, without exception, uphold the values
progressive people actually care about.
